# Northstar Institute of Technology — Career and Placement Policies

## §1 Career Services

Career Services coordinates eligible recruitment activities, employer communication, placement registration, and selected career-development programs.

Students are responsible for reading employer-specific job descriptions before applying.

## §2 Placement Eligibility

Placement eligibility can depend on academic standing, degree program, graduation timeline, disciplinary status, and employer-specific criteria.

An institute-level eligibility rule does not guarantee eligibility for every employer because employers may impose additional criteria.

## §3 Recruitment Applications

Students should submit applications through the channel specified by Career Services or the employer.

Students must provide accurate academic and contact information.

## §4 Placement Offers

A student who accepts an offer should follow the institute's reporting and withdrawal procedures.

Students should not assume that accepting an employer offer automatically resolves all academic or graduation requirements.

## §5 Internship and Placement Distinction

An internship is not necessarily a placement offer.

A student may complete an internship without receiving a full-time offer, and a placement process may occur independently of an internship.

## §6 Career Events

Career Services may conduct technical workshops, mock interviews, employer sessions, and recruitment events.

Attendance at a career event does not itself create an employment commitment.
