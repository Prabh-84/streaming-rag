# Northstar Institute of Technology — Academic Policies

## §1 Academic Terms

NIT organizes academic activity into defined academic terms. Policies may specify an effective term because deadlines, fees, eligibility conditions, and assessment procedures can change between terms.

Students should include the academic term when asking about deadlines or term-specific procedures.

## §2 Course Withdrawal

A student may request withdrawal from a registered course until the withdrawal deadline published for the applicable academic term.

For the 2026 Fall term, the standard course-withdrawal deadline is 20 September 2026 at 23:59 IST.

A withdrawal request must be submitted through the student academic portal. Requests submitted after the deadline require review by the Academic Affairs Office and are not automatically approved.

A course withdrawal does not erase the student's prior enrollment history from the academic record.

## §3 Consequences of Withdrawal

A withdrawal changes the student's course-registration status according to the applicable academic rules. It should not be confused with cancellation of all academic records associated with the course.

Before requesting withdrawal, students should consider prerequisite chains, credit requirements, scholarship conditions, and graduation planning.

Academic advising may help a student understand these consequences, but advising does not replace the formal withdrawal submission.

## §4 Grade Improvement

A student may apply for grade improvement in an eligible course when the course is offered under the grade-improvement rules for the applicable term.

For 2026 Fall, grade-improvement requests must be submitted before the deadline announced by Academic Affairs.

The higher grade is not guaranteed. The result is determined according to the assessment rules for the course.

Students should verify course eligibility before registering for a grade-improvement attempt.

## §5 Re-examination

A student who meets the eligibility requirements for a supplementary or re-examination opportunity must follow the examination schedule and registration procedure published by Academic Affairs.

The re-examination process does not automatically change every component of the original course assessment. The applicable examination notice determines which components are reassessed.

## §6 Academic Appeals

Students may submit an academic appeal through the designated academic office.

An appeal should identify the course, decision being challenged, supporting evidence, and requested review.

Submitting an appeal does not automatically suspend an academic deadline unless the relevant office explicitly grants an extension.

## §7 Credit Requirements

Students are responsible for completing the credit requirements specified by their degree program.

Credits may include required courses, electives, laboratories, projects, internships, and other components defined by the applicable curriculum.

Students should use the academic portal and official curriculum documents when checking their individual progress.

## §8 Prerequisites

Some courses require successful completion of prerequisite courses before registration.

A student should not assume that informal permission from another student or an unofficial timetable entry overrides a prerequisite requirement.

Prerequisite exceptions, when available, are handled by the designated academic authority.

## §9 Academic Standing

Academic standing may depend on academic performance, completed credits, failed courses, and other conditions defined by institute policy.

Students whose standing is affected by an academic decision should consult the applicable notice because the precise conditions may differ by program and term.

## §10 Policy Questions

When two academic documents appear to conflict, students should identify the document's effective term, issuing office, and scope.

A generic handbook statement should not automatically override a later term-specific academic notice.
