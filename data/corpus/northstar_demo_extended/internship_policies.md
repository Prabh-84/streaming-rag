# Northstar Institute of Technology — Internship and Experiential Learning Policies

## §1 Internship Registration

Students participating in an institute-recognized internship must register the internship through the designated academic or career-services process when required.

Registration should identify the organization, internship period, role, supervisor, and other information requested by the institute.

An internship offer by itself does not automatically establish academic credit.

## §2 Eligibility

Eligibility for an academic internship depends on the student's program, academic standing, completed credits, and the conditions stated in the internship notice.

Students should verify eligibility before accepting an internship that requires academic registration.

## §3 Internship Reporting

Students receiving academic credit may be required to submit an internship report, supervisor evaluation, attendance record, or presentation.

The exact submission format and deadline are defined by the applicable internship notice.

## §4 Internship Evaluation

An internship may be evaluated using a combination of supervisor feedback, student documentation, presentation, project outcomes, and faculty review.

The evaluation method should be communicated before or during the internship process.

## §5 Internship Extension

An extension changes the internship period and should be reported to the responsible office when the internship is registered for academic purposes.

Students should not assume that an employer-approved extension automatically extends an academic registration.
