# Northstar Institute of Technology — Laboratory and Safety Policies

## §1 Laboratory Attendance

Students are expected to attend scheduled laboratory sessions for courses that include laboratory components.

A laboratory component may have attendance or completion requirements in addition to the general course attendance requirement.

Students should consult the course outline for the specific laboratory requirement.

## §2 Laboratory Safety

Students must follow laboratory safety instructions, use required protective equipment, and report unsafe conditions to the laboratory supervisor.

Unauthorized operation of equipment is prohibited.

## §3 Equipment

Students must use laboratory equipment only for authorized academic activities.

Equipment faults should be reported rather than repaired by students unless the laboratory supervisor explicitly authorizes the repair.

## §4 Practical Assessments

A practical assessment may evaluate preparation, procedure, observations, results, documentation, and other criteria stated in the course outline.

The assessment criteria for a specific course take precedence over generic laboratory guidance when the two documents describe different assessment details.

## §5 Incident Reporting

Any injury, equipment damage, chemical spill, electrical incident, or other safety event should be reported immediately according to the laboratory's emergency procedure.

Students should not conceal an incident because of concern about penalties. Prompt reporting allows the laboratory to secure the area and provide appropriate assistance.
