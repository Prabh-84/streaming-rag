# Northstar Institute of Technology — Residential and Hostel Policies

## §1 General Hostel Access

Residents must carry their institute identification when entering residential facilities.

Hostel access may be restricted during officially announced maintenance, safety, emergency, or inspection periods.

Residents are responsible for complying with hostel-specific notices issued by the Residential Life Office.

## §2 Visitor Registration

Day visitors may enter the hostel only after being registered at the hostel reception desk and presenting the identification information required by reception.

Visitors must leave the hostel by 21:00 unless an exception has been approved by the Residential Life Office.

Residents are responsible for the conduct of their visitors while they are inside the hostel.

Visitor registration is not equivalent to permission for an overnight stay.

## §3 Overnight Visitors

An overnight visitor requires prior approval from the Residential Life Office.

Registration at reception alone does not authorize an overnight stay.

The resident must submit the overnight-stay request before the visitor arrives. Approval may be denied when accommodation capacity, safety requirements, or residential restrictions prevent the stay.

The resident remains responsible for the visitor during the approved stay.

## §4 Hostel Leave

Residents leaving campus overnight must submit an outpass request through the residential portal before departure.

The request should contain the expected departure date, expected return date, destination or reason where requested, and required emergency contact information.

An approved outpass does not authorize a resident to violate academic, examination, or other institute obligations.

## §5 Emergency Departure

Emergency departures should be reported to the hostel supervisor as soon as reasonably possible.

Emergency situations may use a different approval sequence from ordinary planned leave. Residents should provide supporting information when requested by Residential Life.

## §6 Room Changes

Room-change requests are reviewed by Residential Life.

A resident must not move to a different assigned room without written approval.

Approved room changes are recorded in the residential system. A resident who changes rooms without authorization may be required to return to the assigned room while the request is reviewed.

## §7 Quiet Hours

Residential quiet hours are 22:00 to 06:00 unless a residence-specific notice specifies otherwise.

During quiet hours, residents should avoid activities that create unreasonable noise in rooms, corridors, common areas, or shared study spaces.

## §8 Common Facilities

Residents must leave common kitchens, study rooms, recreation areas, and laundry facilities in usable condition.

Equipment or furniture must not be removed from common facilities without authorization.

Damage should be reported to Residential Life promptly.

## §9 Safety and Restricted Items

Residents must follow fire-safety instructions and must not obstruct emergency exits.

Cooking, electrical equipment, and other potentially hazardous activities are subject to residence-specific safety notices.

When a safety notice conflicts with an ordinary convenience rule, the current safety notice governs the immediate safety procedure.

## §10 Residential Complaints

Residents may submit complaints about maintenance, safety, noise, room allocation, or visitor issues through the Residential Life portal.

Urgent safety issues should be reported directly to the hostel supervisor rather than waiting for routine ticket processing.
