# Northstar Institute of Technology — Library Policies

## §1 Borrowing

Undergraduate students may borrow up to five standard books at one time unless a collection-specific rule limits borrowing.

A library account must be active and linked to the student's institute identity.

Reference materials may have different borrowing restrictions.

## §2 Loan Period

The standard loan period for eligible books is 14 days.

The due date shown in the library system is the operational record for the item.

A student should check the due date rather than relying only on the original checkout date.

## §3 Renewals

Eligible books may be renewed when no reservation has been placed by another user and the item is not subject to a non-renewable collection rule.

A renewal changes the due date displayed in the library system.

## §4 Overdue Items

An overdue item may result in a library fine according to the current library fee schedule.

Students with restricted library accounts may be unable to borrow additional items until the restriction is resolved.

## §5 Lost or Damaged Materials

Students should report lost or damaged materials to the library as soon as possible.

Replacement charges and processing fees depend on the applicable library schedule and material type.

## §6 Digital Resources

Library authentication may be required to access licensed digital resources.

Students should use the official library portal rather than sharing institutional credentials with another person.

## §7 Library Questions

When a question concerns a specific fine, due date, or account restriction, the student's library account is more authoritative than a general handbook statement.
