# Northstar Institute of Technology — Student Grievance and Appeals Policy

## §1 Purpose

The grievance process provides a formal route for students to raise concerns about academic, residential, administrative, or service-related decisions.

Students should provide specific facts and supporting documents rather than relying only on informal descriptions.

## §2 Initial Resolution

Where appropriate, a student should first contact the office responsible for the decision.

The student should retain copies of submissions, responses, and relevant evidence.

## §3 Formal Grievance

A formal grievance should identify the student, issue, relevant date, office or decision involved, supporting evidence, and requested resolution.

The receiving office may request clarification.

## §4 Escalation

If the initial review does not resolve the issue, the student may use the escalation route specified for that category of grievance.

Different categories may have different reviewing authorities.

## §5 Deadlines

A grievance should be submitted within the deadline specified by the applicable policy or decision notice.

If no specific deadline is stated, the student should submit the grievance promptly after becoming aware of the decision.

## §6 Confidentiality

Grievance information should be handled through authorized institutional channels.

Students should avoid posting personal records, credentials, or confidential evidence in public forums when submitting a grievance.
