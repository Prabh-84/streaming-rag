# Northstar Institute of Technology — Scholarship Policies

## §1 Scholarship Eligibility

Scholarship eligibility depends on the requirements of the applicable scholarship program.

Requirements may include academic performance, enrollment status, financial documentation, attendance, or other conditions specified in the scholarship notice.

## §2 Scholarship Renewal

Renewal is not automatic unless the applicable scholarship rules explicitly state otherwise.

Students should complete renewal actions by the published deadline and provide updated documentation when requested.

## §3 Academic Performance

Some scholarships require a minimum academic performance level for renewal.

The applicable scholarship notice determines the exact threshold and the academic period used for evaluation.

Students should not substitute a general institute CGPA rule for a scholarship-specific requirement.

## §4 Loss or Suspension

A scholarship may be suspended or discontinued when a student no longer satisfies the applicable conditions.

Students should consult the scholarship office for the effective date and appeal process when a scholarship decision is disputed.

## §5 Appeals

A student may appeal a scholarship decision through the procedure stated in the scholarship notice.

An appeal does not automatically extend a scholarship payment or registration deadline unless an authorized office grants an extension.
