# Northstar Institute of Technology — Fee Payment Policies

## §1 General Fee Payment

Students are responsible for paying institute fees by the deadline displayed for their academic term.

The student portal is the authoritative place for the student's current invoice and payment status.

A payment submitted close to the deadline should be verified in the portal after the transaction is completed.

## §2 Spring 2026 Late Payment Policy

For the Spring 2026 academic term, the late payment fee was ₹500 when an eligible invoice remained unpaid after its published deadline.

This policy applied to Spring 2026 invoices governed by the Spring 2026 fee notice.

The Spring 2026 amount should not be automatically applied to invoices belonging to another academic term.

## §3 Fall 2026 Late Payment Policy

For the Fall 2026 academic term, the late payment fee is ₹1,000 when an eligible invoice remains unpaid after its published deadline.

This policy applies to Fall 2026 invoices governed by the Fall 2026 fee notice.

The Fall 2026 amount should not be retroactively applied to historical Spring 2026 invoices.

## §4 Resolving Policy Differences

The Spring 2026 and Fall 2026 policies contain different late-payment amounts because they apply to different academic terms.

When answering a late-fee question, the applicable academic term must be established before selecting the amount.

If the term cannot be established from available context, the answer should identify both documented amounts and state that the applicable term is required.

## §5 Payment Reconciliation

A successful payment transaction may take time to appear in the student portal.

Students should retain the transaction reference generated by the payment provider until the portal reflects the payment.

If the payment remains unresolved after the institute's published reconciliation window, the student should contact the Finance Office using the transaction reference.

## §6 Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the type of payment, applicable academic rules, and the reason for the refund.

A refund request does not automatically mean that the full amount will be returned.

The Finance Office may request supporting documents before processing a refund.

## §7 Fee Waivers

A fee waiver is valid only when issued by an authorized office or under an applicable scholarship or institutional program.

Students should not assume that a verbal statement or an informal email automatically changes an invoice.

The official student portal and authorized financial notice should be used to verify the final payable amount.
