# Solstice Center

Solstice Center is a fictional venue that exists only in this synthetic benchmark corpus.

## Capacity

Solstice Center seats up to 200 guests in theatre layout and 140 guests in banquet layout.

## Cancellation Policy

A booking at Solstice Center can be cancelled free of charge up to 21 days before the event. Cancellations inside 21 days forfeit the deposit.

## Catering

Solstice Center offers full in-house catering and does not permit external caterers.
